## Artifact Boundary

Allowed now: artifact reviewers can reproduce the current bundle with `make PYTHON=/data/conda_envs/SpatialQuantization/bin/python e11-full`, or inspect the narrower steps `make PYTHON=/data/conda_envs/SpatialQuantization/bin/python e11-paper-assets`, `make PYTHON=/data/conda_envs/SpatialQuantization/bin/python e11-paper-pdf`, and `make PYTHON=/data/conda_envs/SpatialQuantization/bin/python e11-check`.

Blocked now: preferred pdflatex/bibtex/xelatex clean-checkout reproducibility, any v5 predictive-condition upgrade, any unqualified natural-null or counterexample wording, and any broad optimizer-performance claim.
